A code-drawn newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026's world news in one 1024x1024 image: the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, the Kerch Bridge hit again overnight, Iran's strikes on US sites in Kuwait, Ebola passing 11,500 cases, a sliding Dow, Cyclone Xavier, and an In Brief strip. Pure Python standard library; `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to the output directory and verifies it is a PNG under 1 MiB.

## What it shows

- Lead: Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen — the first-ever three-way summit of the three nuclear powers.
- Kerch Bridge hit again overnight; Iran hits US sites in Kuwait; Ebola tops 11,500 cases; the Dow falls 561; Cyclone Xavier batters the Cook Islands.
- In Brief: Anthropic's Nasdaq debut as ANT, a M5.3 quake in Kyushu, the US AI-chip cloud rule published in the Federal Register, and the Lynch superyacht report.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
